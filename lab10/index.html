Design Reflection

I extended the lab 9 visualization since that was my most recent favorite. Actually, I didn't realize the true benefits of these modalities for this visualization until I added some and played around with them, especially hand gestures. With thumbs up and down, the user can more easily identify the extreme GDP countries. Area and color encoding can show that but this is even clearer and easier for the user. And then adding the thumbs right and thumbs left I realized was basically like a more easily controlled animation! Users can swipe through the order without having to digest the encoding and it makes summarizing and comparing much more convenient. Changing the scale was also nice because a user doesn't have to scroll up and manually change it, but compare with a simple change of fingers! This makes comparing the scales easier but then I realized not scrolling up and down for any of these gestures and just absorbing the content is a big help. Voice I did a similar addition with the scale but I did like that it's easier to group highlights with voice, like I couldn't figure out how I would highlight Asia with hand gestures. The gesture I found the most trouble with was gaze. It would jump around a lot and I realized it does better in identifying my eyes moving side to side rather than up to down. I often had to scroll or move my pointer anyway which kind of defeated the purpose of eye gaze. Nonetheless, it was a really cool modality and was fun to pair with voice. Maybe I could move my head or hand to the side to indicate that it needs to shift my gaze somewhere? Thus combining with camera gestures. As you can see, still a lot to have fun with. This was a fun end to the labs! Will miss this class.
